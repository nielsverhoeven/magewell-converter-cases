## 0. The ask, restated precisely

Add ventilation grilles to the **lid** of all 8 shipped cases. The **floor stays closed** — no
change to `mcc_shell_base()`'s floor, `cradle.scad`, or `mounts.scad`. Keep the existing far-wall
intake and end-wall/fan exhaust untouched. New lid vents must:

- clear the 6 captive-thumbscrew holes,
- clear the tongue-and-groove band,

- ship behind a `cfg.lid_vents` switch, default **true**,

- come with updated goldens (justified) and a `preview-top.png` (+ iso) per case.

## 1. Thermal reasoning

### 1.1 Where the vents belong, relative to the plenum and the fan

The plenum over every device is real and currently empty: `mcc_shell_lid()`'s cavity ends at
`z_top = H − MCC_LID_T` (lid underside), and `mcc_case_layout()` already exposes `z_dev_hi` (device
top). The plenum is `z_top − z_dev_hi`, which the pre-implementation gate hand-computed at **10.8 mm
on the Plus chassis, 10.85 mm on the compact chassis**
(`.claude/knowledge/layout-patch-wall.md` §16.2 item 4) — and confirmed *nothing is drawn in it*
today: the cradle deck and far-flank ribs stay below `z_dev_lo`/`z_dev_lo+MCC_CRADLE_RIB_H`, no
patch-flank rib is emitted on any current SKU, and `mcc_shell_lid()` is a flat slab whose only
downward feature is the T&G groove. That plenum is exactly where the device's hottest surface (its
own metal top) sheds heat by natural convection, and it currently has **no dedicated exit** — the
existing far-wall exhaust band (`z ∈ [32,44]`) partially overlaps it in Z but sits on the *side* wall,
not overhead.

**Placement decision: centre the lid vent field over the device footprint** —
`x ∈ [x_dev_lo, x_dev_hi]` in the case's own X, `y` centred on `y_dev_c` — rather than hugging the
far (−Y) wall's edge. Three independent reasons, all sourced:

1. **Directly over the heat source is the textbook chimney placement.** Buoyancy pulls hot air
   straight up off the hottest surface; venting overhead where the plenum already collects that air
   is the shortest, least-resistance exit for exactly the air that needs to leave.
2. **It avoids short-circuiting the existing far-wall intake.** `knowledge/design/thermal-guidelines.md`
   §5 (verified, FanACDC): *"Avoid short circuits in airflow. Don't let air move straight from intake
   to exhaust without passing over your electronics."* The far-wall intake band sits low at
   `y = −W/2` (case coords); if the new lid vents also hugged that same −Y edge, incoming air could
   rise a few mm and exit almost where it entered, bypassing the device entirely. Centring the field
   on `y_dev_c` (which sits well inboard of the far wall — `y_dev_c = y_dev_lo + dev_w/2`, and
   `y_dev_lo = −W/2 + MCC_WALL + MCC_GAP_FAR` already pushes the device ~19 mm off the wall) forces
   intake air to rise **and** cross laterally over the device before it can escape through the lid.
3. **It does not compete with the fan's own intake path.** R20 (`architecture.md`) already found
   that at the default `fan_y = y_dev_c`, the +X fan **pulls from the plenum over/around the device**,
   not through the 16 mm far-wall duct. A lid vent field centred on the same plenum is therefore
   *serving the same air mass the fan already draws from*, not opening a competing shortcut around
   it — on Plus-family SKUs (`fan = true`) this is a second, passive path off the same hot pocket the
   fan already exhausts, which is a reliability win given R5's own warning that a fan whose
   thermostatic switch never closes "is indistinguishable, from outside, from a fan that has
   failed" — passive lid venting keeps *some* convective escape live even then, exactly the chimney
   benefit `thermal-guidelines.md` §5 credits ("provides some cooling even if the fan stops"). This
   uniform treatment (same module, same field, on all 8 SKUs regardless of `fan`) is a design call
   the architect gate should explicitly bless or override — flagged as **PLAN-ASSUMPTION A** (§5).

**Filter policy:** none. `thermal-guidelines.md` §6 (verified): *"Always place your filter on the
air inlet, not the outlet."* The lid field is exhaust-only; unlike the existing intake bands (which
get a mesh-filter pocket per `layout-patch-wall.md` §5), the lid vents need no filter provision.

### 1.2 How much net lid vent area

The ticket cites the existing R20/T1-30 methodology as the sizing precedent. T1-30 sizes the far-wall
+ −X intake bands against the fan aperture's own circular area
(`MCC_VENT_AREA_RATIO * (π/4) * MCC_FAN_APERTURE_D²` = 1.0 × π/4 × 38² ≈ **1134 mm²**), citing
`thermal-guidelines.md` §3: *"size vent free area comfortably larger than the fan's inlet/outlet
duct area."* That heuristic is about ensuring vents are never the bottleneck relative to a fixed
physical reference (the fan aperture) — it generalizes directly to a new top exhaust path even though
this one exists on every SKU (fan or not), because **the fan bay is reserved on every SKU regardless
of `fan`** (`architecture.md` §6 reservation rule) — T1-30 itself already applies uniformly to all 8
for the same reason.

**Target: net lid vent free area ≥ `MCC_LID_VENT_AREA_RATIO × (π/4) × MCC_FAN_APERTURE_D²`, same
1134 mm² reference point, `MCC_LID_VENT_AREA_RATIO = 1.0` assumed** — enforced by an in-model assert
(§3.3), not a test-only echo (D15 in `layout-patch-wall.md` §16.4 flagged exactly this class of
mistake — "T1-30 lives only in a hard-wired test" — as a defect pattern; the new assert must not
repeat it).

### 1.3 Dust and drop, and why straight through-slots are the v1 answer

- **Upward-facing slots collect dust and admit rain directly** — the ticket's own framing, and
  `fdm-rugged-enclosure-guidelines.md` §13 confirms slotted vents give efficient airflow "while
  minimizing" (not eliminating) dust intake, with no fetched source quantifying slot-vs-louvre
  numerically. A true louvre (angled fins) needs a sloped, oriented print feature that this repo's
  flat-lid / flat-plate print convention does not currently support without a real redesign (the lid
  is a uniform 3 mm slab printed flat, per §2.4), and there is no sourced dust-ingress number to size
  a louvre against.
- **This project's use case is touring/stage, not outdoor rain exposure** (`CLAUDE.md`: "used in live
  performance"). Given that, and given this repo's own "coupons before cases" ethos
  (`CLAUDE.md` non-negotiables), the pragmatic v1 answer is: **accept straight vertical through-slots,
  no louvre**, and record the trade explicitly rather than silently deciding it. This is
  **PLAN-ASSUMPTION B** (§5) — a genuine judgment call for the architect/user to confirm, not
  something to bury in code.
- **Drop/impact:** `fdm-rugged-enclosure-guidelines.md` §12 (recessed-connector principle, already
  governing the patch-wall panel per `architecture.md` §5) generalizes here as "don't perforate near
  an edge that takes impact load." The field's placement (centred over the device, well inboard of
  every wall — see §2.2 margins) already keeps it away from the corners and edges a drop loads first;
  no additional bezel/rim is proposed for v1.

## 2. Geometry

### 2.0 Print orientation — no bridging concern, no reorientation needed

The lid is a flat `MCC_LID_T = 3.0 mm` slab (`lib/mcc/shell.scad:458-489`, confirmed by the existing
lid goldens, e.g. `tests/golden/pro-convert-for-ndi-to-hdmi.lid.json` → `bbox.size = [193.9, 159.85,
3.0]`) and prints flat, its 3 mm thickness running along the printer's Z (build) axis — the same
convention the base already uses. A through-slot cut along that same axis (from the lid's underside,
`z = z_top`, to its outer face, `z = z_top + MCC_LID_T`) is present in **every** print layer at the
same XY footprint — it is a cookie-cutter opening, not a bridge. There is no "reliable bridge span"
concern (`fdm-rugged-enclosure-guidelines.md` §7, ≤10 mm) because nothing spans the opening
horizontally; the guideline's own §13 conclusion — *"print vent slots oriented vertically through the
wall thickness where possible (avoids relying on bridging)"* — is satisfied by construction. No
change to which face is printed down; the module works identically regardless.

### 2.1 Slot pattern

**Rectangular slot field, 2 rows, slots long-axis in Y (short dimension of the case).** Concretely:

| Constant | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_W` | **2.0 mm** (X) | Ticket's own example figure; also `fdm-...md` §13's nearest verified analogue (0.8 mm min feature / 1.0 mm lattice gap) times a safety factor for a field this large. `assumed`. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L` | **20.0 mm** (Y) | Ticket's own example figure. `assumed`. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_WEB_W` | **1.6 mm** | Matches the existing `MCC_VENT_WEB_W` figure/rationale (`layout-patch-wall.md` §5) — kept as its own named constant per this repo's convention (`MCC_LID_T` is numerically `MCC_WALL` but still separately named), so a future change to the wall-vent web doesn't silently move the lid geometry. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP` | **6.0 mm** (Y, between the 2 rows) | Solid lid material between rows; §2.5 optional stiffening rib lives here if added later. `assumed`. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS` | **2** | Keeps the field's Y-extent (46 mm, see §2.1.1) comfortably inside the margin computed in §2.2 on every SKU; a hex field or 3+ rows were considered and rejected for v1 as unnecessary added geometry complexity for marginal area gain — recorded as **PLAN-ASSUMPTION C**. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN` | **5.0 mm** | Inset from `x_dev_lo`/`x_dev_hi` to the field's own X bounds — keeps the field strictly inside the device's own axial footprint (§2.2). `assumed`. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R` | **10.0 mm** | Defensive exclusion radius around every `lid_fastener_pos` (counterbore head ⌀8 → r=4, plus ≥2 mm minimum edge material per `fdm-...md` §8, rounded up for margin). `assumed`. |
| `MCC_LID_VENT_AREA_RATIO` | **1.0** | Mirrors `MCC_VENT_AREA_RATIO`; see §1.2. `assumed`. |

**Slot orientation rationale (long axis in Y, not X):** the lid's longest span (up to 211.5 mm) runs
in X, and `architecture.md` R9 flags ASA warp on a long footprint as the material's main risk. Slots
long in Y (perpendicular to the long/warp-prone axis) remove less continuous material along X than
the reverse orientation would, leaving more material to resist bending along the axis that most needs
it. This also matches the existing wall-vent convention (`vents.scad`'s far-wall slots already run
vertically, i.e. perpendicular to that wall's own long horizontal run).

#### 2.1.1 Field footprint (per-SKU values are computed at render time; formulas are the deliverable)

```
l          = mcc_case_layout(dev, cfg)
x_dev_lo, x_dev_hi, y_dev_c, side_bolt_x  = struct_val(l, ...)   // already-published fields
z_top      = struct_val(l, "H") - MCC_LID_T                       // lid underside; H is a published field

field_x_lo = x_dev_lo + MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN
field_x_hi = x_dev_hi - MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN
field_h    = MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS * MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L
           + (MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS - 1) * MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP        // = 2*20 + 1*6 = 46.0 mm
row_y0(row) = (y_dev_c - field_h/2) + row * (MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L + MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP)
```

**Worked example — `pro-convert-for-ndi-to-hdmi` (the normative template, compact family, fan=false),
using the already-shipped values in `tests/golden/pro-convert-for-ndi-to-hdmi.*.json` and
`layout-patch-wall.md` §16.1/§16.2:**

- `dev_l = 100.9`, so `field_x_lo/hi = x_dev_lo+5 / x_dev_hi-5`, an **90.9 mm** run.
- `y_dev_c ≈ −30.8` (derived, §2.2), `field_h = 46.0` → field spans `y ∈ [−53.8, −7.8]`, i.e. still
  well inboard of both long walls.
- Slot columns per row at pitch `2.0+1.6=3.6 mm`: `floor((90.9+1.6)/3.6) = 25` columns, minus the
  side-bolt strip exclusion (§2.3, ~2 columns lost) → **~23 slots/row**.
- Area per row ≈ `23 × 2.0 × 20.0 = 920 mm²`; **2 rows ≈ 1840 mm²**, comfortably over the
  1134 mm² target (§1.2), even after the side-bolt exclusion.
- On the Plus family (`dev_l = 117.5`, run 107.5 mm, ~28 slots/row after exclusion) the same
  arithmetic gives **≈ 2240 mm²** — also comfortably over target.

These are hand-checks for sanity, exactly as `layout-patch-wall.md` §16 did for the original 8-SKU
gate — **the render + the module's own assert are the authority** (§4 step 5); do not hand-tune the
constants to hit a specific number if the assert already passes.

### 2.2 Keep-out margins — why this placement clears everything by construction

The field, as bounded above, is nowhere near any of the four listed keep-outs, and the plan requires
an explicit assert for each (not just a comment) so a future SKU or measurement change fails loudly
instead of silently:

1. **Groove band / wall / perimeter.** The field's X bounds sit inside `[x_dev_lo, x_dev_hi]`, which
   is itself inset from the shell walls by the end-zone allowances (40–47 mm, `architecture.md` T1-09)
   — an order of magnitude more than the groove band's own footprint (≈2.1 mm, straddling the
   interior-cavity boundary at each wall, `_mcc_tg_frame()` in `shell.scad`). The field's Y bounds
   are centred on `y_dev_c`, which per §2.1.1's worked example sits **~46 mm** inside the nearer
   (far, −Y) wall on the tightest SKU — again far clear. **Add the assert anyway** (§3.3) rather than
   relying on "it happens to be far enough": a future device record with a very long/offset device
   could close that margin.
2. **6 thumbscrew holes.** All 6 `lid_fastener_pos` entries sit at a **10 mm inset from an outer
   edge** (`MCC_FASTENER_INSET = 10.0`, `layout-patch-wall.md` §6) — 4 corners plus 2 long-wall
   mid-spans, none of them ever inside the device-centred field (the far-wall mid-span sits at
   `y = −(W/2−10)`, computed at ≈ −69 to −73 mm across the 8 SKUs — outside the field's Y-range of
   roughly `y_dev_c ± 23`, a ≥15 mm clearance on the tightest SKU). **Assert it explicitly** (§3.3)
   using an inflated-rectangle test against every `lid_fastener_pos`, radius
   `MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R`.
3. **Gussets.** The 6 lid-fastener gusset webs (`_mcc_gusset_web()` in `shell.scad`) are a
   **base-only** feature — they run `z ∈ [MCC_FLOOR_T, z_top]`, i.e. they never reach the lid's own Z
   range (`[z_top, z_top+MCC_LID_T]`) at all. No lid-side geometry can physically collide with a
   gusset; the reason to still keep the field clear of the gusset's XY footprint is purely so a future
   underside stiffening rib (§2.5) has room, which the fastener keep-out radius above already buys.
4. **Side-bolt keep-out strip.** `side_bolt_x = struct_val(l, "side_bolt_x")` sits **inside** the
   field's X-run on every current SKU (it equals `x_dev_c`, the device's own X centre, under the
   `pos [0,0]` placeholder every device file currently carries). This is the one keep-out that
   genuinely intersects the field and needs a real cut exclusion, not just an assert — see §2.3.

### 2.3 The side-bolt exclusion — a real notch, not just an assert

Reuse `mcc_side_bolt_keepout()`'s own `strip_w` (7.0 mm, already published by `fasteners.scad` and
already consumed once in `vents.scad`'s `_mcc_far_wall_excl()`) as the exclusion width, centred on
`side_bolt_x`, applied to **every row** of the lid field (it is a full-X-column exclusion, not
Z/row-dependent, since the underlying hazard — a service technician's screwdriver sightline down onto
the far-wall boss, and keeping the lid's structure solid directly above the case's one horizontal
fastener axis — is not row-specific):

```
excl = [[side_bolt_x - sb_strip_w/2, side_bolt_x + sb_strip_w/2]]   // sb_strip_w = struct_val(mcc_side_bolt_keepout(), "strip_w")
```

Any slot whose own `[c − slot_w/2, c + slot_w/2]` footprint overlaps `excl` is **dropped entirely**,
matching the existing `_mcc_vent_slot_centers()` convention (`vents.scad:24-42`) — narrowing a slot
instead of dropping it is explicitly not this repo's pattern.

### 2.4 Stiffening ribs — deferred, not shipped in v1

The ticket asks for these only conditionally ("if the perforated field reduces lid stiffness"). Two
reasons to defer rather than add them now:

1. **Print-orientation constraint.** The lid's outer (cosmetic) face is the one carrying the 6
   thumbscrew counterbores and is the face that prints against the bed (matching the base's existing
   "prints with its functional/interior face open" convention) — a rib can only be added on the
   **underside** (interior-facing) face, which is the side that consumes `MCC_LID_CLEAR` plenum
   margin. That margin has real slack today (T1-14 passes with **8.8 mm** of headroom on Plus, more
   on compact — `layout-patch-wall.md` §16.2 item 1(a)/`architecture.md`), so a shallow rib (≤2 mm)
   is not blocked, but it is a second feature this ticket did not ask to be mandatory.
2. **This repo's own "coupons before cases" rule** (`CLAUDE.md` non-negotiables) argues for measuring
   whether the perforated 3 mm field actually sags/cracks under normal handling before adding a rib
   pre-emptively.

**Decision for v1: ship without stiffening ribs.** `MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP` (6.0 mm solid strip between
the two rows) is sized to leave room for a rib later without any constant renaming. If the printed
lid (Tier-4 milestone, `CLAUDE.md` "Next milestone is physical") shows a stiffness problem, add
`mcc_lid_vent_ribs_add(dev, cfg)` as an additive module unioned into `mcc_shell_lid()` before the
`difference()`, height ≤ `MCC_LID_CLEAR`-safe (re-check T1-14 with the rib's protrusion subtracted
from the plenum), in a follow-up ticket — do not add it speculatively now.

### 2.5 What does NOT change

- `mcc_shell_base()` — untouched. The floor stays closed (explicit ticket constraint).
- The existing far-wall intake/exhaust bands and `mcc_vent_intake_area()`/T1-30 — untouched; this is
  an additive top exhaust, not a resize of the existing wall vents.
- `MCC_LID_CLEAR`, `MCC_TG_W`, `MCC_TG_H`, `MCC_CLR_TG` — untouched (v1 ships no rib; §2.4).
- Device data files (`lib/mcc/devices/*.scad`) — untouched; the field is derived purely from
  `mcc_case_layout()`'s existing published fields.

### 3.2 `lib/mcc/vents.scad`

1. **Generalize `_mcc_vent_slot_centers()`** to accept the slot/web width as parameters, defaulting
   to the existing globals, so the wall-vent call sites need no change:

   ```openscad
   function _mcc_vent_slot_centers(run_lo, run_hi, excl_ranges = [],
                                    slot_w = MCC_VENT_SLOT_W, web_w = MCC_VENT_WEB_W) =
       let(
           pitch = slot_w + web_w,
           run = run_hi - run_lo,
           n = floor((run + web_w) / pitch),
           used = n * pitch - web_w,
           start = run_lo + (run - used) / 2 + slot_w / 2,
           all = [for (i = [0:1:n - 1]) start + i * pitch]
       )
       [for (c = all) if (!_mcc_in_any_range(c - slot_w / 2, c + slot_w / 2, excl_ranges)) c];
   ```

   Every existing call site (`mcc_vents()`, `mcc_vent_intake_area()`) is unaffected — they call it
   positionally with 3 args, picking up the same defaults as before.

2. **Add `mcc_lid_vent_area(dev, cfg)`** (public function, mirrors `mcc_vent_intake_area()`'s own
   pattern — computed from the exact same slot-centre lists the cut module draws, so the assert and
   the geometry can never disagree):

   ```openscad
   // Function: mcc_lid_vent_area()
   // Usage:
   //   area = mcc_lid_vent_area(dev, cfg);
   // Description:
   //   T1-36 (proposed, see plan §4 step 7): total NET lid-vent free area, mm^2, over both rows,
   //   computed from the SAME slot-centre list mcc_lid_vents_cut() draws.
   function mcc_lid_vent_area(dev, cfg) =
       let(
           l = mcc_case_layout(dev, cfg),
           x_dev_lo = struct_val(l, "x_dev_lo"), x_dev_hi = struct_val(l, "x_dev_hi"),
           side_bolt_x = struct_val(l, "side_bolt_x"),
           sb_strip_w = struct_val(mcc_side_bolt_keepout(), "strip_w"),
           field_x_lo = x_dev_lo + MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN,

                                                    MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_W, MCC_LID_VENT_WEB_W))
   ```

3. **Add `mcc_lid_vents_cut(dev, cfg)`** (public module, SUBTRACTIVE):

   ```openscad
   // Module: mcc_lid_vents_cut()
   // Usage:
   //   mcc_lid_vents_cut(dev, cfg);
   // Description:
   //   SUBTRACTIVE. Cuts a 2-row field of vertical through-slots in the lid slab, centred over the
   //   device's own footprint (over the plenum -- plan docs/plans/2026-09-09-lid-vents.md §1.1),
   //   offset from the far-wall/±X-end-wall chimney slots this file already cuts in mcc_vents().
   //   Called ONLY from mcc_shell_lid() (issue #24 -- the floor stays closed; NEVER call this from
   //   mcc_shell_base()), gated on cfg["lid_vents"] (default true).
   // Arguments:
   //   dev = device record.
   //   cfg = variant-config assoc-list.
   module mcc_lid_vents_cut(dev, cfg) {
       l = mcc_case_layout(dev, cfg);
       L = struct_val(l, "L"); W = struct_val(l, "W"); H = struct_val(l, "H");
       x_dev_lo = struct_val(l, "x_dev_lo"); x_dev_hi = struct_val(l, "x_dev_hi");
       y_dev_c  = struct_val(l, "y_dev_c");
       side_bolt_x = struct_val(l, "side_bolt_x");
       lid_pos  = struct_val(l, "lid_fastener_pos");
       z_top = H - MCC_LID_T;
       sb_strip_w = struct_val(mcc_side_bolt_keepout(), "strip_w");

       field_x_lo = x_dev_lo + MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN;
       field_x_hi = x_dev_hi - MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN;
       field_h = MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS * MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L
               + (MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS - 1) * MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP;
       field_y_lo = y_dev_c - field_h / 2;
       field_y_hi = field_y_lo + field_h;
       excl = [[side_bolt_x - sb_strip_w / 2, side_bolt_x + sb_strip_w / 2]];

       // --- Assert set: see plan §3.3 for the full rationale of each ---
       assert(field_x_hi > field_x_lo,
           str("mcc: lid vent field has non-positive X run on \"", mcc_dev_slug(dev), "\""));
       assert(field_x_lo >= -L / 2 + MCC_WALL + MCC_TG_W + MCC_CLR_TG + 1.0
           && field_x_hi <= L / 2 - MCC_WALL - MCC_TG_W - MCC_CLR_TG - 1.0,
           str("mcc: lid vent field X-range ", [field_x_lo, field_x_hi],
               " intrudes on the T&G groove band on \"", mcc_dev_slug(dev), "\""));
       assert(field_y_lo >= -W / 2 + MCC_WALL + MCC_TG_W + MCC_CLR_TG + 1.0
           && field_y_hi <= W / 2 - MCC_T_PATCH - MCC_TG_W - MCC_CLR_TG - 1.0,
           str("mcc: lid vent field Y-range ", [field_y_lo, field_y_hi],
               " intrudes on the T&G groove band or the patch wall on \"", mcc_dev_slug(dev), "\""));
       for (p = lid_pos)
           assert(p[0] < field_x_lo - MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R
               || p[0] > field_x_hi + MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R
               || p[1] < field_y_lo - MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R
               || p[1] > field_y_hi + MCC_LID_VENT_FASTENER_KEEPOUT_R,
               str("mcc: lid fastener ", p, " is inside the lid vent field keep-out on \"",
                   mcc_dev_slug(dev), "\""));
       assert(MCC_LID_VENT_WEB_W >= 1.6,
           "mcc: MCC_LID_VENT_WEB_W below the 1.6 mm minimum web width");

       area = mcc_lid_vent_area(dev, cfg);
       area_min = MCC_LID_VENT_AREA_RATIO * PI / 4 * MCC_FAN_APERTURE_D * MCC_FAN_APERTURE_D;
       assert(area >= area_min,
           str("mcc: lid vent net area ", area, " mm^2 below threshold ", area_min,
               " mm^2 on \"", mcc_dev_slug(dev), "\""));

       for (row = [0 : 1 : MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS - 1]) {
           y_lo = field_y_lo + row * (MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L + MCC_LID_VENT_ROW_GAP);
           for (c = _mcc_vent_slot_centers(field_x_lo, field_x_hi, excl,
                                             MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_W, MCC_LID_VENT_WEB_W))
               translate([c - MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_W / 2, y_lo, z_top - MCC_EPS])
                   cube([MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_W, MCC_LID_VENT_SLOT_L, MCC_LID_T + 2 * MCC_EPS]);
       }
   }
   ```

   `mcc_dev_slug(dev)` already exists (used throughout `shell.scad`'s own asserts) — `vents.scad`

## 5. PLAN-ASSUMPTIONs (escalate before/at the architect gate)

- **PLAN-ASSUMPTION A — uniform lid-vent treatment on fan and non-fan SKUs alike.** §1.1 argues a
  device-centred lid vent field is a *benign* complement to the Plus family's fan (both draw from the
  same overhead plenum), not a competing shortcut. This is a physical-reasoning call, not a measured
  one — no airflow simulation or physical test backs it. If the architect or user wants the Plus
  family's lid field reduced/repositioned to avoid drawing makeup air away from the fan's own duct
  path, that is a parameter change to `MCC_LID_VENT_ROWS`/`MCC_LID_VENT_END_MARGIN` per family, not a
  structural rework — flag it now rather than after two independent geometries exist.
- **PLAN-ASSUMPTION B — no louvre/dust mitigation in v1.** §1.3. Straight through-slots are proposed
  as the pragmatic first cut, explicitly deferring a louvre design pending a stated dust/rain exposure
  requirement that does not currently exist in `CLAUDE.md`'s fixed decisions. If the user's actual
  usage includes outdoor/rain exposure, this needs revisiting before print.
- **PLAN-ASSUMPTION C — 2 rows of 2×20 mm slots, not a hex field.** §2.1. The ticket offered both as
  examples; the rectangular tiling was chosen because it reuses `_mcc_vent_slot_centers()` (already
  generalized for this purpose, §3.2) with no new geometry primitive, and comfortably clears the area
  target (§2.1.1). A hex field is not ruled out for a future revision if the aesthetic or a measured
  dust result argues for it.
- **All new constants (§3.1) are `assumed`**, same confidence tier as the existing `MCC_VENT_*`
  family — none of them are sourced dimensions from `knowledge/**`; they are engineering choices
  consistent with this repo's existing vent-slot precedent. Flag per `CLAUDE.md`'s "never invent a
  dimension" rule: these are not invented *device* dimensions, they are new *design* parameters, the
  same category as `MCC_VENT_SLOT_W`/`MCC_VENT_WEB_W` already are.
- **T1-36/T1-37 are proposed assert numbers, not yet ratified.** §4 step 12 — the architect gate
  should confirm the numbering (in case another in-flight change already claims them) before they are
  written into `layout-patch-wall.md` §9's canonical table.
