## Type / interfaces
Audio interface — same physical jacks as AES67 (3.5 mm TRS unbalanced IN/OUT, 4.4 mm TRRRS balanced
IN/OUT), Dante 8-channel network audio instead of AES67.

## Physical
- Housing family: [Compact/TX](../housing-families.md#b-compacttx-1009--602--233-mm)
- Dimensions: 100.9 × 60.2 × 23.3 mm (same chassis as AES67)
- Weight: unknown (retailer figure "1.76 lb" is the boxed/packaged weight, not the device weight — unreliable)

## Port layout
- Audio end: unbalanced 3.5 mm TRS IN, then OUT; then balanced 4.4 mm TRRRS IN, then OUT
- Network end: RJ45 "ETH+POE" and USB-B "+5V/USB+POWER"
- 16-position rotary switch on the edge near the network end
- 1/4"-20 hole near the audio end (diagram labels it "Screw Mounting Hole")
- Bottom: central rubber pad + two screw heads (estimated from image)
- LEDs: Power, PTP
- No Kensington slot, no reset button

## Power
- PoE: IEEE 802.3af
- DC: 5 V via USB-B; bundled adapter 5 V/2.1 A
- Max power: ~5 W

## Thermal
- Operating temperature: –10–50 °C
- Fan: no (fanless)

## Mounting
- 1/4"-20 hole ("Screw Mounting Hole") near the audio end; L-bracket #92240 in box; Fishtail bracket also compatible
- **Not compatible** with the ACC1001x rack mounting kits despite sharing the Compact/TX chassis

## Other notes
In box: 4.4 mm-to-dual-XLR-M and -F cables, USB A-to-B cable, power adapter. No official datasheet
PDF found for this model (404) — tech-specs page and manual were used instead.
