# Pro Convert IP to AIO 4K

## Type / interfaces
IP/NDI decoder — HDMI 2.0 + 12G-SDI BNC output simultaneously, 4K60.

## Physical
- Housing family: [IP decoder](../housing-families.md#c-ip-decoder-120--793--245-mm) — same body as
  IP to HDMI / IP to HDMI 4K, with an added BNC connector for the SDI output
- Dimensions: 120 × 79.3 × 24.5 mm
- Weight: unknown (retailer figure ≈200 g conflicts with the (unpublished) official spec — treat as unreliable)

## Port layout
- Front (short face), left to right: PWR LED (blue), SIG LED (blue), PGM LED (red), PVW LED (green), rotary encoder push-knob
- Rear (short face), left to right: HDMI OUT → SDI OUT BNC → USB HOST (USB 3.0 Type-A) → CONFIG (USB-C, power-capable) → audio OUT 3.5 mm → ETH/PoE RJ45 with 2 LEDs
- Top: two clusters of louvered vent slots
- Rear panel: 4 corner Phillips screws
- No Kensington slot, no visible reset button, single RJ45

## Power
- PoE: IEEE 802.3at only
- DC: USB-C, 12 V, up to 20 W
- Max power: ~15.1 W — highest of any model in the whole Pro Convert range

## Thermal
- Operating temperature: 0–40 °C
- Fan: no — fanless, relies on the two louvered top vent clusters for passive convection; given this
  is the highest-power model in the range, vent clearance is especially important for a case design

## Mounting
- 1/4"-20 threaded hole; also documents Φ6.5 mm mounting holes (unique to this model among the IP-decoder family)
- Fishtail Bracket ships in the box: part STR00211, with PAR10040 (thumbscrew-adapter fasteners), MECO0067 (M4×12 black screws ×2), MECO0068 (M4 nuts ×2)

## Other notes
In box: USB-C-to-C cable (ELE00114) and USB-C-to-A cable (ELE00066), both 80 cm; universal power
adapter ELE00125.
