Retailer domains are listed where a report used them as a fallback for an unpublished figure (always
flagged unverified in the model/table it appears in) — no retailer page content beyond the single

fetching in this research pass (blocked by anti-bot protection); no data from these retailers is

taken from manual figures/photos); enclosure alloy not stated anywhere; L-bracket #92240 dimensions
unpublished; Fishtail Bracket installation guide is a vector PDF (not text-extractable), only the STL
is directly usable.
