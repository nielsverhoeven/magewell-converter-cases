## Note on tooling

Per the research brief, no binary files were *deliberately* downloaded to disk. Several WebFetch calls
against Neutrik's own PDF/DXF drawings could not be parsed as text by WebFetch and were automatically
cached locally by the tool as a side effect; those cached copies were then opened with the Read tool
(which can render PDF pages as images) purely to extract the dimension text already cited above, and
were not redistributed. The canonical source for every dimension remains the neutrik.com / RS Online
URL listed, not the local cache path.
